### [MECHANISM — 0:36–1:10]

*[Shift to B-roll of hair follicle animation or microscopy imagery]*

**"Here's the science — simplified. Your hair follicles go through cycles: growth, rest, shed, regrow. But in androgenetic alopecia — pattern hair loss — follicles slowly stop cycling. They go dormant. They fall asleep."**

**"PP405 targets something called the Mitochondrial Pyruvate Carrier — or MPC — inside your hair follicle stem cells. These stem cells are still there. They haven't died. They've just... stopped."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Follicle stem cells = still alive. Just dormant."]*

**"PP405 blocks MPC. That blockage causes a build-up of a molecule called pyruvate. That triggers a metabolic shift — your cells switch to a process called glycolysis — basically, they generate more fuel. And that extra fuel is what kickstarts the stem cells back into action."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "MPC Inhibitor → More Cellular Energy → Stem Cells Reactivate"]*

**"In plain English: it doesn't just feed your hair. It reboots the factory."**

---

### [THE STUDY — 1:11–1:55]

**"Now let's talk about the study — because this is where I want to give you the real picture."**

**"Pelage ran a Phase 2a clinical trial. 78 men and women with androgenetic alopecia. Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled — that's the gold standard structure. Half got PP405 gel, applied once a day for 4 weeks. Half got a placebo. Then they were monitored for 12 weeks total."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "78 participants | Randomised | Double-Blind | Placebo-Controlled"]*

**"The headline result: 31% of men with more advanced hair loss saw greater than a 20% increase in hair density at week 8. Compared to zero percent in the placebo group."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "31% vs 0% — Hair Density Increase >20% at Week 8"]*

**"And here's the part that genuinely surprised researchers: PP405 grew new hairs in follicles that were completely bare. That's regeneration — not just preservation."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "New Hair in Previously Bare Follicles — True Regeneration"]*

**"Oh — and no systemic side effects. No absorption into the bloodstream. Clean safety profile."**

---

### [HONEST VERDICT — 1:56–2:35]

**"So. Is this the cure? Let me be straight with you."**

**"78 people is a small sample size. The 31% figure comes from a subgroup — men with more advanced loss — not every participant. The treatment was only applied for 4 weeks. And we haven't seen the full peer-reviewed paper yet."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Small sample | Subgroup result | Short dosing period | Phase 2a only"]*

**"This is Phase 2 of what is typically a 3-to-4 phase process. Phase 3 trials are planned for 2026. If everything goes well, we're realistically looking at 2027 to 2029 before this reaches a pharmacy shelf."**

*[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Phase 3 planned 2026 | Potential approval: 2027–2029"]*

**"BUT — and this is a big but — the mechanism is genuinely novel. The safety is clean. The trial design is solid. And regrowing hair in a bald spot? That hasn't been done by a topical treatment before. Not like this."**

**"This isn't wishful thinking. This is early, promising, legitimate science. And it's worth watching very closely."**
